About Us

Built on Trust,
Delivered with Precision

Swift Atlantis Contracting L.L.C was established on 15 September 2022 under Commercial License No. 1080914 to undertake construction and contracting activities in accordance with approvals granted by the Department of Economy and Tourism and Dubai Municipality.

The company operates as a main contractor, delivering fully integrated construction solutions from excavation and foundation works through project completion, handover, and issuance of Building Completion Certificates.
